Recovery Match

Manual-confirm stock recovery, not a risky marketplace.

Healthy surplus stock can be matched to nearby opted-in stores. No escrow, no payments, no delivery, no regulated categories in v1.

Rule 1Both sides approve

No contact reveal until buyer and seller both show intent.

Rule 2Founder review

Early matches are manual ops learning, not full automation.

Rule 3Safe only

No near-expiry, cold-chain, medicine, baby formula, frozen, meat, cosmetics.

Rule 4No liability trap

NazmOS does not handle payment, delivery, or invoices.
